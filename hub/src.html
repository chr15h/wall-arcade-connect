How it works

Credits
50 plays for 0.005 ETH, paid on Ethereum mainnet. Each run uses 1 credit. One balance covers all of NFC's games. Star Runner and Sky Hopper are SuperRare's own games and use their own credits.
Practice
Free, and not recorded.
Scores
The platform replays your inputs on its server and records the score. The browser never sends a score.
Weekly prizes
One leaderboard per game for the week: Friday 15:00 to Friday 15:00, Lisbon time. The first week runs from Thursday Oct 22, 00:00 to Friday Oct 30, 15:00. Prizes are handled by the organizer.
Paid but no credits?
Open the game's wallet menu, then "purchase help".

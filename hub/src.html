The wall

A real painted wall in Lisbon for NFC Summit 2027. Every brick is a person.

The credits

One SuperRare Connect sign-in. NFC's cabinets share one balance of credits. A pack is paid in USDC on Ethereum; each run uses one credit.

The scores

The browser only sends your moves. The platform replays them on its server and records the score: no score ever comes from the page.

How it works

4 steps

This path is for developers. Not one? Skip to the bottom: DM @NFCsummit and we put your game online with you.

1
Copy the Connect Kit

One button. It tells your AI how a SuperRare Connect game works, what the server verifier must guarantee, and where the wall's data lives.

2
Adapt SuperRare's template

You need: Node.js 22 or newer, pnpm (10.11, the template's package manager) and the rare-profile CLI (beta: npm i -g @rareprotocol/rare-profile@beta).

› Node 22+› pnpm 10.11› rare-profile beta

Clone the public Studio games template, give your AI the kit, and describe the game. It rewrites three things that must agree: the rules (a deterministic engine), the verifier that replays your players' moves, and the screen.

“Adapt this template into a memory game where I have to find the two halves of each name. Keep the verifier strict and deterministic.”
OPEN THE TEMPLATE ↗
3
Test it, then build it

Run the template's own checks (typecheck, tests, the local verifier exercise), then build the archive. Nothing is paid and nothing is public until you deploy.

› pnpm test› verifier exercise› pnpm build → game.zip
4
Put it online, get a cabinet

Create a free Studio account, deploy with the rare-profile CLI, publish your verifier and scoring event (the runbook has every command). Send your Studio link to @NFCsummit: your cabinet opens here.

Joining NFC's credit group means one treasury: NFC's. Your players share one balance with NFC's other cabinets, and the USDC goes to NFC. To be paid directly, create your own credit group with your own treasury instead.

The deal

    

      
Your game, your name on it.

      
Players sign in and pay through SuperRare Connect: credits are bought in packs of USDC, one credit per run. The money goes to the treasury of the credit group, never through our code.

      
Cabinets in NFC's group share one balance and NFC's treasury. To be paid directly, run your own credit group with your own treasury: your credits then stay with your game.

      
The platform's server decides every score. Prizes and payouts are manual, by the organizer of the group.

      
If your game stars a specific name from the wall, its holder gets an edition. Every game carries the credit line and a link back to the wall.

   "sprite": { "id": "…", "url": "https://…/people/sprites/<id>.png", "cols": 4, "rows": 4, "frame": 48 },
   "updatedAt": "2026-10-27T13:23:20.157Z" }, …]
One object per name painted on the wall. "sprite" is null until that person makes a character.
A sheet: PNG 192 × 192, transparent, 4 × 4 frames of 48 px.
Row = direction: 0 up · 1 right · 2 down (facing you) · 3 left. Column = step: 4 steps, 130 ms each.
Draw: ctx.drawImage(img, step*48, row*48, 48, 48, x, y, w, h), with img.crossOrigin = "anonymous".
Keep the pixels sharp: ctx.imageSmoothingEnabled = false (CSS: image-rendering: pixelated).
Display only: a sprite is never part of a score.
